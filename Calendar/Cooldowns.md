# Cooldowns

Every fixed waiting period you're currently inside, with the exact unlock date.

Filled automatically by the `cooldown` routine and read by `daily-brief` on every
run. **Triviality is not a filter** — a username change cooldown, a free trial
ending, a returns window, a warranty, a cashback deadline all get a row.

Dates get computed with a real command, never estimated:
`date -d "<start> +N days" +%F`

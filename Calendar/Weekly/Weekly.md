# Weekly

Output of the `weekly-review` routine. One note per ISO week, `YYYY-Wnn.md`.

The point is catching things that slipped, so it's meant to be blunt: what
actually finished, what you said you'd do and didn't, which projects went
untouched, and what to drop.

> [!note] This folder stays empty for most people
> `weekly-review` is the routine that gets skipped. If you've gone a month
> without one, either put it in a recurring scheduled task or admit you won't do
> it and delete the routine.

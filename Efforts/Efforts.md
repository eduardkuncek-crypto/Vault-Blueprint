> [!tip] Replace this table with a Base
> Once you have more than about five projects, make `Efforts.base` and embed it
> with `![[Efforts.base]]` — projects by status, days open, untouched 7+ days. A
> hand-maintained table goes stale and that's how projects quietly disappear.

Allowed `status:` values:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| `active` | Being worked on now |
| `planned` | Decided, not started |
| `upcoming` | Starts on a known date |
| `stalled` | **Has a real next action and nothing is happening.** Different problem from parked |
| `parked` | Deliberately off. Not a failure |
| `done` | Finished |

`stalled` vs `parked` is the distinction worth keeping. One needs a nudge, the
other needs leaving alone.

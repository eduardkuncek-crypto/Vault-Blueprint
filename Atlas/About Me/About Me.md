# About Me

The long version of [[me]]. **One note per subject** — never one long file.

`me.md` is the summary an AI reads every session; these are the detail it opens
when it needs them. When the two disagree, these notes are newer.

## Suggested notes

Not a required list. Make the ones that match your life, ignore the rest, invent
others.

`Identity` · `Family` · `Friends and social` · `Daily routine` · `Energy and
focus` · `How I learn` · `Tech skill inventory` · `Working with AI` ·
`Preferences and tastes` · `Opinions` · `Money` · `Food` · `Health` · `Fitness` ·
`Languages` · `Music` · `Reading` · `Habits and quirks` · `Future` ·
`Things to confirm`

Two are load-bearing and worth writing early:

- **`Working with AI.md`** — how you want an AI to behave. Read at the start of
  every session, unconditionally.
- **`Preferences and tastes.md`** — with a **Named favourites** table. This is
  the first place anything checks when asked "what's my favourite X", and the
  reason it can answer instead of guessing.

## Worth creating early

The AI is told to look for these by name, so they earn their place:

- **`My machines.md`** — every device, specs, OS, what it's for
- **`Home network.md`** — router, ISP, wifi, anything static
- **`My software stack.md`** — what you actually run
- **`My online accounts.md`** — what exists, what's unverified, what you pay for

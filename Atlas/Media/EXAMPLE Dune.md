Frank Herbert, 1965. About 400 pages in, just past the dinner party chapter.

## What I like about it

The politics are the actual story and the sandworms are set dressing, which is
the opposite of what I expected going in. Every conversation is three
conversations — what's said, what's meant, and what both people know is being
tested.

The dinner party chapter is the best thing I've read this year. Nothing happens.
It's fifteen pages of people being polite at each other and it's unbearable in a
good way.

Not a review — the reason it caught me.
